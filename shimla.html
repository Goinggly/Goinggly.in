Highlights

Why visit Shimla

The experiences travellers love most — all easy to build into your trip.

Kalka–Shimla toy train

A UNESCO-listed narrow-gauge ride through tunnels and pine forests.

The Ridge & Mall Road

Colonial architecture, cafés and the town's evening buzz.

Christ Church

A neo-Gothic landmark on the Ridge, one of North India's oldest churches.

Kufri

Snow fun in winter, pony rides and viewpoints the rest of the year.

Mashobra & Naldehra

Quiet cedar forests, apple orchards and a historic golf course.

Viceregal Lodge

The grand former residence of the British Viceroy.

FAQs

Good to know

When does it snow in Shimla?

Snowfall is usually seen from late December to February, though it varies each year.

Can Shimla be combined with Manali?

Yes — it's a very popular route, and we arrange the road transfer between them.

How do I reach Shimla?

Most guests fly to Chandigarh and drive about 3.5 hours; the Kalka toy train is a lovely scenic option.
