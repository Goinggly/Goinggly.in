Highlights

Why visit Pattaya

The experiences travellers love most — all easy to build into your trip.

Coral Island (Koh Larn)

Speedboat to white-sand beaches with water sports and clear water.

Alcazar cabaret show

A glittering, world-famous live production for all ages.

Sanctuary of Truth

A towering all-wood temple carved right on the sea.

Nong Nooch Garden

Tropical gardens, elephant show and cultural performances.

Floating Market

Shop, snack and paddle through a market built on water.

Walking Street evenings

Seafront dining, music and the famous neon strip after dark.
