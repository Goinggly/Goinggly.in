Highlights

Why visit Pattaya

The experiences travellers love most — all easy to build into your trip.

Coral Island (Koh Larn)

Speedboat to white-sand beaches with clear water and water sports.

Alcazar Cabaret Show

A glittering, world-famous live production for all ages.

Sanctuary of Truth

A towering all-wood temple carved right on the sea.

Nong Nooch Tropical Garden

Manicured gardens, an elephant show and cultural performances.

Underwater World Pattaya

Walk through glass tunnels beneath sharks, rays and tropical fish.

Walking Street

Seafront dining, music and the famous neon strip after dark.
