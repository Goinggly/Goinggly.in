Highlights

Why visit Manali

The experiences travellers love most — all easy to build into your trip.

Solang Valley

Skiing in winter; paragliding, ropeway and zorbing in warmer months.

Atal Tunnel & Sissu

Drive through the world's highest tunnel to the Lahaul valley.

Rohtang Pass

High-altitude snow views (seasonal; permit-based and weather-dependent).

Hadimba Temple

A wooden temple set inside a quiet deodar forest.

Old Manali

Riverside cafés, bohemian lanes and live music.

Beas River Valley

Riverside drives and picnic spots along the Beas, with rafting in season.

FAQs

Good to know

Is Rohtang Pass always open?

No. It is seasonal, weather-dependent and needs a permit, so we plan Solang and the Atal Tunnel as dependable alternatives.

When is the best time for snow?

Typically December to February, with high-altitude snow lingering into spring.

Is Manali safe for families?

Yes, with well-planned stops and trusted local drivers; we tailor the pace to your group.
