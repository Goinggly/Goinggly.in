Day-by-day itinerary · 6N / 7D

  1. Day 1

    Arrive in Shimla

    Pick-up from Chandigarh and drive to Shimla (approx. 3.5 hrs). Check in and take an evening walk on Mall Road and the Ridge. (Optional: arrive by the UNESCO Kalka–Shimla toy train, subject to availability.)

  2. Day 2

    Shimla sightseeing

    Jakhu Temple, Christ Church on the Ridge, Mall Road and the Viceregal Lodge (Indian Institute of Advanced Study).

  3. Day 3

    Kufri & Mashobra

    Day trip to Kufri for viewpoints, pony rides and the Himalayan Nature Park (snow activities in winter), then the cedar forests of Mashobra.

  4. Day 4

    Shimla → Manali

    Free morning for last-minute shopping on Mall Road. Check out and drive to Manali (approx. 7–8 hrs), with stops at Pandoh Dam and Beas river viewpoints. Check in and unwind. Evening at Mall Road and Hadimba Temple.

  5. Day 5

    Solang Valley adventure

    Solang Valley for snow activities and skiing in winter, or paragliding, ropeway and zorbing in warmer months.

  6. Day 6

    Atal Tunnel & Sissu

    Drive through the Atal Tunnel to the Lahaul valley and Sissu. Subject to road, tunnel and weather conditions; Rohtang Pass is permit-based and seasonal.

  7. Day 7

    Departure

    Free morning for a walk through Old Manali's cafés. Breakfast, check-out and drop for your onward journey.

FAQs

Good to know

Is Rohtang Pass always open?

No. It is seasonal, weather-dependent and needs a permit, so we plan Solang and the Atal Tunnel as dependable alternatives.

When is the best time for snow?

Typically December to February, with high-altitude snow lingering into spring.

Is Manali safe for families?

Yes, with well-planned stops and trusted local drivers; we tailor the pace to your group.
