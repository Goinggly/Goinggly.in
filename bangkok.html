Highlights

Why visit Bangkok

The experiences travellers love most — all easy to build into your trip.

Grand Palace

Thailand's most spectacular royal complex, home to the Emerald Buddha.

Wat Pho

Bangkok's oldest temple and the giant Reclining Buddha.

Chao Phraya dinner cruise

Skyline views, live music and a buffet gliding down the river.

Damnoen Saduak Floating Market

Paddle-boat vendors, fresh fruit and street snacks on the water.

Safari World & Marine Park

A drive-through safari with dolphin and stunt shows — great for families.

Mahanakhon Skywalk

Step onto a glass floor over 300 m above the city for Bangkok's best skyline view.

Sample itinerary

5 days in Bangkok

A starting point — we tailor every day to your dates, pace and budget.

Day 1

Arrive & riverside evening

Airport pick-up, hotel check-in, then an easy evening at Asiatique with a Ferris-wheel view.

Day 2

Temples & the old city

Grand Palace, Wat Pho and Wat Arun, followed by a street-food trail through Chinatown.

Day 3

Markets & a dinner cruise

Floating-market morning, free afternoon for malls or a Thai massage, then a Chao Phraya dinner cruise.

Day 4

Shop, spa & skyline

Chatuchak or MBK shopping, a rooftop sunset and a farewell dinner.

Day 5

Departure

Breakfast and airport drop with plenty of time to spare.
