Day-by-day itinerary · 4N / 5D

  1. Day 1

    Arrive in Bangkok

    Arrive at Bangkok airport, meet our representative and transfer to your hotel. Evening is free — try the riverside Asiatique night bazaar or Chinatown street food (optional).

  2. Day 2

    Grand Palace, Wat Pho & Wat Arun

    Explore the Grand Palace and the giant Reclining Buddha at Wat Pho, then cross the river to Wat Arun. Evening street-food trail in Yaowarat (Chinatown). Dress modestly — shoulders and knees covered at temples.

  3. Day 3

    Floating market & Chao Phraya dinner cruise

    Morning visit to a traditional floating market (Damnoen Saduak or Amphawa). Free afternoon, then a Chao Phraya River dinner cruise with skyline views.

  4. Day 4

    Safari World & shopping

    Full-day Safari World and Marine Park — drive-through safari, dolphin and stunt shows. Evening shopping at MBK or Siam Paragon.

  5. Day 5

    Departure

    Enjoy a free morning for last-minute shopping or a Thai massage. Breakfast, check-out and airport transfer for your flight home.

Day-by-day itinerary · 5N / 6D

  1. Day 1

    Arrive Bangkok & drive to Pattaya

    Arrive at Bangkok airport and drive to Pattaya (approx. 2 hrs). Check in and unwind. Evening at Pattaya Beach Road; optionally catch the Alcazar cabaret show.

  2. Day 2

    Coral Island (Koh Larn) by speedboat

    Speedboat to Coral Island for white-sand beaches, with optional parasailing, jet-ski and banana-boat rides. Buffet lunch on the island, back in Pattaya by evening.

  3. Day 3

    Pattaya city sights

    Sanctuary of Truth, Big Buddha Hill, Pattaya Viewpoint and a gem gallery stop. Evening at Walking Street (optional).

  4. Day 4

    Pattaya → Bangkok

    Enjoy a leisurely breakfast and a last stroll along the beach. Check out and drive to Bangkok (approx. 2 hrs). Evening is free — try the riverside Asiatique night bazaar or Chinatown street food (optional).

  5. Day 5

    Grand Palace, Wat Pho & Wat Arun

    Explore the Grand Palace and the giant Reclining Buddha at Wat Pho, then cross the river to Wat Arun. Evening street-food trail in Yaowarat (Chinatown). Dress modestly — shoulders and knees covered at temples.

  6. Day 6

    Departure

    Enjoy a free morning for last-minute shopping or a Thai massage. Breakfast, check-out and airport transfer for your flight home.

Day-by-day itinerary · 6N / 7D

  1. Day 1

    Arrive in Bangkok

    Arrive at Bangkok airport, meet our representative and transfer to your hotel. Evening is free — try the riverside Asiatique night bazaar or Chinatown street food (optional).

  2. Day 2

    Grand Palace, Wat Pho & Wat Arun

    Explore the Grand Palace and the giant Reclining Buddha at Wat Pho, then cross the river to Wat Arun. Evening street-food trail in Yaowarat (Chinatown). Dress modestly — shoulders and knees covered at temples.

  3. Day 3

    Bangkok → Phuket

    Enjoy a free morning for last-minute shopping or a Thai massage. Transfer to Bangkok airport for your flight to Phuket (approx. 1 hr 25 min). Check in and catch the sunset at Kata or Karon beach.

  4. Day 4

    Phi Phi Islands by speedboat

    Full-day speedboat trip to Phi Phi with snorkelling, beaches and lunch on the island. Maya Bay access depends on current park rules; the national park fee (about 400 THB) is paid locally.

  5. Day 5

    Phang Nga Bay & James Bond Island

    Cruise through the limestone towers of Phang Nga Bay, sea-canoe into caves and visit James Bond Island and a floating village.

  6. Day 6

    Phuket city tour & Promthep Cape sunset

    Big Buddha, Wat Chalong and Old Phuket Town's Sino-Portuguese streets, then sunset at Promthep Cape.

  7. Day 7

    Departure

    Enjoy a relaxed morning at the beach before check-out. Breakfast, check-out and airport transfer for your flight home.

Highlights

Why visit Bangkok

The experiences travellers love most — all easy to build into your trip.

Grand Palace

Thailand's most spectacular royal complex, home to the Emerald Buddha.

Wat Pho

Bangkok's oldest temple and the giant Reclining Buddha.

Chao Phraya dinner cruise

Skyline views, live music and a buffet gliding down the river.

Damnoen Saduak Floating Market

Paddle-boat vendors, fresh fruit and street snacks on the water.

Safari World & Marine Park

A drive-through safari with dolphin and stunt shows — great for families.

Mahanakhon Skywalk

Step onto a glass floor over 300 m above the city for Bangkok's best skyline view.
