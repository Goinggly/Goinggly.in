FAQs

Good to know

When is the best time to visit Phuket?

November to April is the dry, calm-sea season and ideal for island trips. Other months are cheaper but weather can affect boat tours.

Can I combine Phuket with Krabi?

Absolutely — Phuket and Krabi are a classic pairing, a few hours apart by road or ferry.

Are island tours included?

Phi Phi and Phang Nga trips can be included in your package; tell us what you want and we'll build it in.
