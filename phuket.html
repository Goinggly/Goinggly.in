Highlights

Why visit Phuket

The experiences travellers love most — all easy to build into your trip.

Phi Phi Islands

Maya Bay, snorkelling and the dramatic cliffs of Phi Phi Leh.

Phang Nga Bay

Limestone towers, sea-canoe caves and James Bond Island.

Big Buddha

Sit above the island with sweeping views in every direction.

Promthep Cape sunset

Phuket's most famous sunset spot, at the island's southern tip.

Old Phuket Town

Sino-Portuguese shophouses, cafés and a Sunday walking street.

Beach-club evenings

Kata, Karon and Patong — dining and sunsets by the sea.

FAQs

Good to know

When is the best time to visit Phuket?

November to April is the dry, calm-sea season and ideal for island trips. Other months are cheaper but weather can affect boat tours.

Can I combine Phuket with Krabi?

Absolutely — Phuket and Krabi are a classic pairing, a few hours apart by road or ferry.

Are island tours included?

Phi Phi and Phang Nga trips can be included in your package; tell us what you want and we'll build it in.
