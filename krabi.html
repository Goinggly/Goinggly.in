Day-by-day itinerary · 6N / 7D

  1. Day 1

    Arrive in Krabi

    Arrive at Krabi airport and transfer to your hotel. Check in. Sunset at Ao Nang beach and an evening stroll through the night market.

  2. Day 2

    Four Islands tour by longtail

    Longtail and speedboat day hopping between Koh Poda, Chicken Island, Koh Tup and Phra Nang Cave Beach with snorkelling and a beach lunch.

  3. Day 3

    Emerald Pool, Hot Springs & Tiger Cave Temple

    Swim in the jungle's blue-green Emerald Pool, soak in the hot springs and climb (optional) to Tiger Cave Temple for sweeping views.

  4. Day 4

    Krabi → Bangkok

    Free morning at the beach or pool. Transfer to Krabi airport for your flight to Bangkok (approx. 1 hr 25 min). Evening is free — try the riverside Asiatique night bazaar or Chinatown street food (optional).

  5. Day 5

    Grand Palace, Wat Pho & Wat Arun

    Explore the Grand Palace and the giant Reclining Buddha at Wat Pho, then cross the river to Wat Arun. Evening street-food trail in Yaowarat (Chinatown). Dress modestly — shoulders and knees covered at temples.

  6. Day 6

    Floating market & Chao Phraya dinner cruise

    Morning visit to a traditional floating market (Damnoen Saduak or Amphawa). Free afternoon, then a Chao Phraya River dinner cruise with skyline views.

  7. Day 7

    Departure

    Enjoy a free morning for last-minute shopping or a Thai massage. Breakfast, check-out and airport transfer for your flight home.

    Arrive Bangkok & drive to Pattaya

    Arrive at Bangkok airport and drive to Pattaya (approx. 2 hrs). Check in and unwind. Evening at Pattaya Beach Road; optionally catch the Alcazar cabaret show.

    Coral Island (Koh Larn) by speedboat

    Speedboat to Coral Island for white-sand beaches, with optional parasailing, jet-ski and banana-boat rides. Buffet lunch on the island, back in Pattaya by evening.

    Pattaya → Bangkok

  6. Day 6

  10. Day 10

FAQs

Good to know

Is Krabi good for honeymooners?

Very. Quiet beaches, private longtail trips and romantic resorts make it a favourite for couples.

Krabi or Phuket?

Phuket has more nightlife and variety; Krabi is calmer and more scenic. Many guests do both.

Is Krabi family-friendly?

Yes, with gentle beaches and easy day trips; we pick suitable stays and activities.
