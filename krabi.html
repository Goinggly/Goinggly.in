Why visit Krabi

The experiences travellers love most — all easy to build into your trip.

Railay Beach

Reachable only by boat, framed by cliffs and a legendary cave beach.

Four Islands tour

Longtail hopping between Tup, Chicken, Poda and Phra Nang.

Emerald Pool & Hot Springs

Swim in jungle pools of striking blue-green water.

Tiger Cave Temple

Climb the stairs for a sweeping view across Krabi's countryside.

Seven Islands sunset tour

A full-day island circuit that ends with a golden-hour sunset.

Ao Nang

Easy beachside dining, shopping and golden evenings.

FAQs

Good to know

Is Krabi good for honeymooners?

Very. Quiet beaches, private longtail trips and romantic resorts make it a favourite for couples.

Krabi or Phuket?

Phuket has more nightlife and variety; Krabi is calmer and more scenic. Many guests do both.

Is Krabi family-friendly?

Yes, with gentle beaches and easy day trips; we pick suitable stays and activities.
