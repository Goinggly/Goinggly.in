Day 219 Nov

Vinpearl Safari - Tickets with Private Transfers

Vinpearl Safari - Tickets with Private Transfers

08 Hours

Have breakfast at the hotel. At 09:00, the tour guide and driver will pick you up and transfer you for sightseeing.Visit Vinpearl Safari Phu Quoc, Vietnam's first semi-wildlife conservation park, offering a unique experience of observing animals in a semi-natural environment. Covering 380 hectares, the park houses over 3,000 animals from 150 different species, including rare animals such as Bengal tigers, African lions, and giraffes. The park is divided into two main areas: the Safari Park, where visitors can experience "people in cages, animals free," and the Open Zoo, which features various animal shows. Continue to Grand World Phu Quoc, known as the city that never sleeps. It is an entertainment, shopping, and festival destination in Southeast Asia, offering exciting experiences throughout 24 hours. Please note that there is no entrance ticket for Grand World. If guests wish to attend any shows or participate in coin-operated games, the charges must be paid directly by the guests.
