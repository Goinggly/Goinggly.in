Day-by-day itinerary · 5N / 6D

  1. Day 1

    Arrive Bangkok & drive to Pattaya

    Arrive at Bangkok airport and drive to Pattaya (approx. 2 hrs). Check in and unwind. Evening at Pattaya Beach Road; optionally catch the Alcazar cabaret show.

  2. Day 2

    Coral Island (Koh Larn) by speedboat

    Speedboat to Coral Island for white-sand beaches, with optional parasailing, jet-ski and banana-boat rides. Buffet lunch on the island, back in Pattaya by evening.

  3. Day 3

    Pattaya city sights

    Sanctuary of Truth, Big Buddha Hill, Pattaya Viewpoint and a gem gallery stop. Evening at Walking Street (optional).

  4. Day 4

    Pattaya → Bangkok

    Enjoy a leisurely breakfast and a last stroll along the beach. Check out and drive to Bangkok (approx. 2 hrs). Evening is free — try the riverside Asiatique night bazaar or Chinatown street food (optional).

  5. Day 5

    Grand Palace, Wat Pho & Wat Arun

    Explore the Grand Palace and the giant Reclining Buddha at Wat Pho, then cross the river to Wat Arun. Evening street-food trail in Yaowarat (Chinatown). Dress modestly — shoulders and knees covered at temples.

  6. Day 6

    Departure

    Enjoy a free morning for last-minute shopping or a Thai massage. Breakfast, check-out and airport transfer for your flight home.

    Speedboat to Coral Island for white-sand beaches, with optional parasailing, jet-ski and banana-boat rides. Buffet lunch on the island, back in Pattaya by evening.

  3. Day 3

    Pattaya → Bangkok

    Enjoy a leisurely breakfast and a last stroll along the beach. Check out and drive to Bangkok (approx. 2 hrs). Evening is free — try the riverside Asiatique night bazaar or Chinatown street food (optional).

  4. Day 4

    Grand Palace, Wat Pho & Wat Arun

    Explore the Grand Palace and the giant Reclining Buddha at Wat Pho, then cross the river to Wat Arun. Evening street-food trail in Yaowarat (Chinatown). Dress modestly — shoulders and knees covered at temples.

  5. Day 5

    Bangkok → Phuket

    Enjoy a free morning for last-minute shopping or a Thai massage. Transfer to Bangkok airport for your flight to Phuket (approx. 1 hr 25 min). Check in and catch the sunset at Kata or Karon beach.

  6. Day 6

    Phi Phi Islands by speedboat

    Full-day speedboat trip to Phi Phi with snorkelling, beaches and lunch on the island. Maya Bay access depends on current park rules; the national park fee (about 400 THB) is paid locally.

  7. Day 7

    Phang Nga Bay & James Bond Island

    Cruise through the limestone towers of Phang Nga Bay, sea-canoe into caves and visit James Bond Island and a floating village.

  8. Day 8

    Phuket → Krabi

    Enjoy a relaxed morning at the beach before check-out. Check out and transfer by road to Krabi (approx. 2.5–3 hrs). Check in. Sunset at Ao Nang beach and an evening stroll through the night market.

  9. Day 9

    Four Islands tour by longtail

    Longtail and speedboat day hopping between Koh Poda, Chicken Island, Koh Tup and Phra Nang Cave Beach with snorkelling and a beach lunch.

  10. Day 10

    Departure

    Free morning at the beach or pool. Breakfast, check-out and airport transfer for your flight home.
